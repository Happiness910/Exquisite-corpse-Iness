# Pat the potato #

This is the story of Pat. He's not a potato. It's just his nickname.
He's actually a rapper. Try to say his name out loud. See ? It's sounds good.
Like a beatbox theme.
One day, Pat was invited to perform at a secret underground show in an abandoned subway station.
When he started rapping, the walls began to vibrate in rhythm, as if the entire city was turning into his instrument.
