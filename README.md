# Pat the potato

This is the story of Pat. He's not a potato. It's just his nickname.
He's actually a rapper. Try to say his name out loud. See ? It's sounds good.
Like a beatbox theme.
One day, while Pat was enjoying his daily morning vanilla (sugar-free, of course) protein shake, a news article retained his attention.  
"Tat the Tomato, the new rap sensation!"  
Pat could not believe his eyes. A competitor? What kind of name is "Tat the Tomato" anyway? Pat quickly swallowed his remaining shake, and ...



He didn't know what he was doing just like a headless chicken from KFC. The weather was great but his mood was somewhat crazy. Though the chickens were delicious.
